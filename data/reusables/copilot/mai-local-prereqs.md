### Prerequisites

You must have:
* A Windows PC
* An NVIDIA GPU
* At least 96 GB of available combined RAM
* At least 60 GB of storage available
* llama.cpp v0.7.0 or later installed and on your PATH. See [the ggml-org/llama.cpp repository](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp) on {% data variables.product.prodname_dotcom_the_website %}.
