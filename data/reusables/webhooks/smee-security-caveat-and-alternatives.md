Smee channels are not authenticated or secure, so anyone with your webhook proxy URL can see the payloads sent to it. Alternatively, you can use a tool like [ngrok](https://ngrok.com/docs/getting-started/), [localtunnel](https://localtunnel.github.io/www/), or the [Hookdeck CLI](https://hookdeck.com/docs/cli). These tools expose your local machine to the internet to receive the payloads. To view payloads in your browser without running a server, use [Hookdeck Inspect](https://inspect.hookdeck.com/webhooks/github).
