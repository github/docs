Capitalized terms not defined in these Terms have the meanings given elsewhere in the Agreement.

## About Partner Success

Partner Success is a proactive support service available as an add-on for Partners with an active subscription to the Partner Support Offering (“Partner Support”). It provides an entitlement of eighty (80) proactive consulting hours per year, prorated for any partial term as set out in the applicable Order Form.

## Subscription Term and Allocation of Hours

Your Partner Success subscription is coterminous with your Partner Support subscription. If your initial Partner Success term is shorter than 12 months, your allocated hours will be adjusted on a pro rata basis to align with your then-current Partner Support subscription end date. For example, if your Partner Support subscription expires on December 31 and you add Partner Success effective July 1 (a remaining term of 184 days), your allocation will be 40 hours, calculated based on the proportion of the year remaining (184/365) and rounded to the nearest whole hour.

Your allocated hours will be specified in the applicable Order Form, and additional hours may be purchased as needed. Prepaid hours may be used for Eligible Services, as defined below and subject to availability. Prepaid hours must be used within the applicable subscription term and will not carry over to any renewal term. Any prepaid fees for unused hours are non-refundable.

## Regional Allocation

Partner must select a single Region for its Partner Success subscription, which will be specified in the applicable Order Form. For purposes of these Terms, "Region" means each of the following: (1) Europe and the Middle East; (2) Asia and Oceania; and (3) North and South America. All prepaid hours allocated under the applicable Order Form, including the eighty (80) hours described above (or any prorated amount), must be consumed in the selected Region and may not be used in, split across, or transferred to any other Region. GitHub will perform the Services in, and source the delivery engineers supporting the Services from, the selected Region.

## Eligible Services

You may use your prepaid hours for any combination of the following Eligible Services:

* **Design Reviews:** Expert feedback on Partner proposals and solution architectures to ensure alignment with best practices.
* **Problem Solving:** Hands-on help with complex Partner challenges or technical setups.
* **Train-the-Trainer:** Enablement sessions to help Partner teams teach and scale GitHub knowledge internally. These sessions are intended for Partner personnel only; end customer participation is not permitted. The maximum number of Partner participants per session is ten (10).
* **Accelerator Co-Creation:** Collaboration to design new assets and accelerators for partner solutions.

## Service Scheduling, Lead Times, and Delivery

For purposes of these Terms, ‘Services’ means the Eligible Services (and any related deliverables) performed by GitHub under Partner Success. Requests to schedule Services must be submitted at least twenty-eight (28) days in advance. GitHub will use commercially reasonable efforts to make resources available within twenty-eight (28) days from the date a request is submitted and ready for scheduling. This lead time supports capacity planning and helps manage resource availability.

If capacity allows, GitHub may provide support sooner, for example, by assigning an engineer on a short-term rotational basis (typically two (2) weeks) or by addressing smaller requests submitted through the designated Partner intake repository on an asynchronous basis.

Any changes to the delivery schedule must be agreed in writing (including by email) by authorized representatives of each party. Services must be requested in minimum increments of four (4) hours and may not exceed eight (8) hours in any single day.

Partner must identify a primary responsible person (the "Primary Responsible Person") who has authority to act on Partner's behalf in matters relating to the Services and must serve as the primary interface between GitHub's project team and Partner departments participating in the Services within that Region.

Each Primary Responsible Person must:

* Obtain and provide accurate and applicable information, data, consents, decisions, and approvals without delay as required by GitHub to perform the Services;
* For Hands-On Delivery, promptly provide access, workspace, and connectivity to the systems and other facilities necessary for GitHub personnel while working at Partner locations, and help resolve and escalate Services issues within Partner's organization as necessary; and
* Identify all Partner team members with appropriate systems access who will act as GitHub's hands-on-keyboard during the Services and otherwise make personnel available to assist GitHub in performing its responsibilities.

Partner is responsible for protecting the privacy and security of its data, including Personal Data, and for controlling access by GitHub personnel and subcontractors. This includes conducting security check-in procedures and providing badges and a personal escort for all on-site engagements.

Partner must:

* Not share administrative credentials with GitHub employees or personnel;
* Create temporary access credentials for use by GitHub employees or personnel that are valid on the Services start date and that automatically expire upon completion of the Services; and
* Provide its security regulations to GitHub. GitHub will ensure that its personnel and subcontractors make commercially reasonable efforts to comply with Partner’s security regulations while at Partner sites or in connection with Partner systems.

Unless otherwise agreed in advance in writing (including by email) by GitHub’s Delivery Manager, GitHub personnel (including subcontractors) will observe Partner’s working hours, working rules, and holiday schedules while working on Partner premises.
